AI authoring is the fastest way into Cloud. It onboards you, learns how your business works, and creates your
datasets for you.

1. **Connect ClickHouse** with a [read-only user](/docs/cloud/clickhouse-users).
2. **Tell it about your business.** The AI reads your schema and asks what matters, such as how you count revenue,
   who your customers are, and how data is split by tenant.
3. **Review the draft.** It proposes datasets with dimensions, measures, relationships, and tenant keys. Accept, edit,
   or reject each one.
4. **Go live.** Accepted datasets are served as [REST](/docs/cloud/rest-api) and [MCP](/docs/cloud/mcp) endpoints.

AI-authored datasets are the same as code-authored ones: they produce the same deployment contract, follow the same
access and tenant rules, and are served the same way.

If you prefer to manage datasets in code, define them in TypeScript or Python and
[deploy from TypeScript](/docs/cloud/deploy-typescript) or [from Python](/docs/cloud/deploy-python) instead.
